Chemical Usage Rules

DO NOT USE:

  • Bilt Hamber Auto-QD (1:20) on Glass, mirrors, or alloy wheels
  • Rain-X on Side mirrors, unpainted black plastics, or bodywork
  • Bilt Hamber Auto-Korrosol on Grill meshes, badges, seals, or hot surfaces
  • Autoglym Car Glass Polish on Plastic trim, window surrounds, or screens

ALWAYS DILUTE:

  • Bilt Hamber Auto-QD: ALWAYS 1:20 (24ml/25g for 476ml/476g Distilled water)
  • Bilt Hamber Surfex-HD: ALWAYS 1:10 (45ml/48g for 455ml/455g tap water) for arches, tyres & jambs, Can be diluted further 1:100 to be used on interior vinyl, dashboard plastics, steering wheels, leather (spot testing first), and light fabric upholstery stains
  • Bilt Hamber Touch-Less: 1:15 for weekly (25ml for 375ml warm tap water), 1:5 for deep clean (67ml for 333ml warm tap water)
  • Bilt Hamber Auto-Wash: 1:2000 (1 tsp/5ml/5g per 10L/10kg warm tap water)

NEVER DILUTE:

  • Autoglym Fast Glass
  • Autoglym Car Glass Polish
  • Rain-X
